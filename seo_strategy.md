# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public landing page (`/`)
- Public content and discovery pages (`/community`, `/academy`, `/academy/course/:id`, `/order-hall`, `/order-hall/:id`, `/contest/:id`, `/activity/:id`)
- Public legal and support pages (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `/support`)
- Shared HTML shell, crawl files, and social sharing metadata that affect public routes

## Out of scope
- Publisher workspace (`/pub/**`)
- OPC workspace (`/opc/**`)
- Admin routes (`/admin/**`, `/screen`)
- Authenticated profile/account routes behind client-side gates (`/profile`, `/portfolios`, `/notifications`, `/account-settings`)

## Target audience
- Chinese-speaking demand publishers and OPC/freelance service providers looking for a project matching platform.
